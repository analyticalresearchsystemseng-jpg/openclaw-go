OpenClaw Go - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 May 2026

Overview

OpenClaw Go is a companion app for OpenClaw users. This privacy policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

  • Gateway Connection: Your OpenClaw gateway URL and access token (stored locally on your device)
  • Location: GPS location is collected only when you actively request it, and is never stored or transmitted to third parties
  • Device Info: Basic device information (model, OS version) for app diagnostics only

How We Use Your Data

  • Location data is used solely for displaying your current position on a map
  • Device info is used to ensure compatibility with your device
  • Gateway credentials are stored securely on your device only

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your OpenClaw gateway or any data it processes.

Third Parties

We do not share your data with any third parties. The app connects directly to your OpenClaw gateway — no intermediary servers.
